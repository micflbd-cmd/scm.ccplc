Insurance Details

Premium Calculation

Total Insurance Val = ((PI Amount + Freight) × Load%) + (PI Amount + Freight), then × Exchange Rate. Marine/WAR SRCC amounts = Total Insurance Val × their % minus any deduction. Stamp Duty = 20 + ((Total Insurance Val − 100,000) / 100,000) × 10. Total Premium = Marine + WAR SRCC + VAT + Stamp Duty + Addendum.
